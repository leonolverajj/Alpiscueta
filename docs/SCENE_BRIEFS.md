# Scene briefs (visual name → what to draw)

Narration for each scene is in `guion/guion.json` (field `text`); read it so visuals land on the
right beats. Times below are fractions of the segment length T.

## Batch A — module `scenes/ch_a.py`
- **empires_fall** (s01): dark backdrop. Three silhouetted angular castles/ziggurat/temple on a ridge
  crumble in turn (pieces fall, dust particles) at 0.1/0.25/0.4. At 0.6 a small glowing book appears
  centre on a stone pedestal; light rays. Captions: none.
- **book_glow** (s02): close-up of an old book with crimson cover; pages flutter; dozens of ink
  handwriting lines; small hands of many different scribes (gloved/skin) reach in from edges across
  time; ends with "¿POR QUÉ?" big in FONT_TITLE at 0.7.
- **lecturer** (s04): stylised theatre stage seen from the audience: back-of-head crowd silhouettes in
  foreground, a lone lecturer (F.person, LECTURER colours, seen small, back-lit) at a lectern under a
  spotlight; on a big screen behind him an old map with a "?". Caption "TORONTO · 2017".
- **explorer_map** (s05): our HERO bust (F.character F.HERO) studying a glowing parchment map with a
  magnifying lens; around the map: icons labelled RAZÓN, BIOLOGÍA, EVOLUCIÓN light up in sequence;
  a misty unlabeled region at the map edge stays dark and mysterious.
- **nietzsche** (s06): F.NIETZSCHE bust, fierce, intense, over a dark-red slab background with a
  mountain silhouette; caption "FRIEDRICH NIETZSCHE · 1844–1900". Later a small telescope/test tube
  icon rises from a cross-shaped glow ("LA CIENCIA").
- **scribes** (s07): monastery scriptorium: rows of hooded monks (F.person kneel/sit) writing at desks;
  a great unified diagram (concentric circles) above them; at 0.7 the diagram turns into a magnifying
  glass/eye of inquiry that turns around to look back at the monks. Caption "MIL AÑOS".
- **xmas_tree** (s08): a cosy room, our HERO (smile→puzzled) decorating a stylised angular fir tree
  with red/gold ornaments; a big "?" floats; snow outside window.
- **broken_statue** (s09): monumental statue head of an old bearded god cracks and splits at 0.2;
  pieces fall into a dark chasm. Left side a black void labelled NIHILISMO, right side marching red
  banners labelled IDEOLOGÍAS; a small HERO figure on a narrow ridge between.
- **marching** (s10): dark backdrop, endless columns of identical grey soldiers marching (F.crowd
  pose walk) under huge banners; a giant crooked statue with missing arm and leg ("religión lisiada")
  limps ahead of them. Caption "SIGLO XX".
- **raft** (s11): our HERO kneeling on a small log raft on a dark sea (waves), a huge shadowy hand
  pulls logs out one by one (0.2, 0.4, 0.6); in the water beneath, faint glowing serpent shapes.
  Captions on logs: "VERDAD", "VALORES", "SENTIDO".

## Batch B — module `scenes/ch_b.py`
- **island_map** (s12): top-down map: a small island (labelled LO QUE SABEMOS), ringed by a misty band
  (LO QUE INTUIMOS), surrounded by a dark ocean (LO DESCONOCIDO · CAOS) with sea-serpent hints. Rings
  draw on sequentially, camera slowly zooms out.
- **argument** (s13): two characters (F.HERO and F.WOMAN) facing each other with angular speech
  bubbles full of jagged scribbles; tension lines; at 0.5 the woman cries (closed eyes, tear drops) and
  a soft glowing shape rises from her chest to her head (the realisation).
- **fog_mediators** (s14): the misty belt: a hooded mystic figure and an artist with a brush stand in
  luminous fog between a bright little island city (left) and a black stormy ocean (right); glowing
  threads link them to both.
- **subpersonalities** (s15): HERO bust in centre; behind him 5 translucent coloured "masks/shadows"
  of himself with different expressions (shout, fear, smile, grim) peel off like cards fanning out.
  Caption "SUBPERSONALIDADES".
- **rage_god** (s16): a towering red armoured war-god spirit (crimson, horned helmet, flaming spikes)
  looms and slowly possesses a small HERO figure (red glow creeping up); caption "MARTE" at 0.75.
- **dreamer** (s17): a sleeping figure (closed eyes) on a bed seen from the side; above, an enormous
  dream cloud with mythic shapes (a dragon, a tower, a hero with sword, a tree) drawn in teal line;
  caption "CARL JUNG". The dream shapes slowly assemble into a bigger lit "idea" lightbulb-like star.
- **carver** (s18): a carver at night by a fire, carving a wooden animal figure (simple angular raven
  /bear shape, generic, not a copy of any specific sacred design); behind him, glowing outlines of
  animals (whale, raven, bear) drift in his dream; two elder spirits (translucent) watch kindly.
- **myth_quote** (s19): dark theatre stage; a lone figure walks along a path that splits into two:
  one lit (gold), one leading to a tragic mask; the quote text appears in FONT_SERIF italic:
  "Todos vivimos un mito, pero muy pocos sabemos cuál." — C. G. Jung.

## Batch C — module `scenes/ch_c.py`
- **museum** (s20): gallery hall, a large glowing Renaissance-style painting (abstract: golden figure
  ascending among clouds), a crowd of visitors in silhouette staring; light from painting on faces.
- **concert** (s21): huge crowd silhouettes with raised arms (F.crowd arm_up), stage lights in teal/
  gold, layered sound-wave patterns (concentric angular waves) pulsing to a beat.
- **hamlet_numbers** (s22): split screen: left, a stage actor holding a skull (classic Hamlet pose,
  original design) under spotlight; right, floating numbers and geometric figures (π, 1, 2, 3, a
  triangle) moving a giant lever that lifts a planet. Caption "FICCIÓN ≠ FALSEDAD".
- **ancestors_chain** (s23): a horizontal timeline with an unbroken chain of silhouettes morphing:
  cell → fish → small mammal → primate → human; caption "3.500 MILLONES DE AÑOS". People watching
  people: eyes drawn above the chain.
- **campfire** (s24): night, group of ancient people around a fire; an elder storyteller gestures; the
  smoke forms shapes of stories (a dragon, a hero, a mother). Caption "10.000 AÑOS".
- **moses_judge** (s25): desert camp at dusk; an elder with staff (F.SAGE) sits as judge between two
  quarrelling men; a queue of people behind; tally marks accumulate in the sky (dozens → hundreds).
- **moses_tablets** (s26): the same elder on a mountain peak holding two stone tablets that blaze with
  light; rays; the tally marks condense into the tablets. Caption "LA LEY".
- **chimp_abstraction** (s27): left: a tribe of chimpanzee silhouettes around a big dominant one;
  middle: several human leaders (kings, chiefs) in a row; right: their shapes merge into a single

- **emperor** (s28): a Roman emperor on a throne with laurel wreath; worshippers bow; a tall glowing
  column labelled "EL PRINCIPIO" towers above him and the emperor himself bows to it at 0.6.
